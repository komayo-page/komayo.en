Chapter 4
The Spell

Seated face to face on the ancient stones of the garden, Jun and the enigmatic stranger enter into a game of ōgi that transcends mere play. For Jun, every move becomes a step in a subtle dance, in which the pieces are only pretexts for prolonging this moment suspended between two worlds.

The woman's presence exerts a magnetic attraction on him. Her gestures, marked by an age-old grace, turn each movement of a piece into a refined act of seduction. Her scarlet lips part now and then to comment on the play, but words seem secondary beside the silent language growing between them.

The game becomes a metaphor for their meeting, each move exchanged weaving an invisible thread between their beings. Jun watches his partner with growing fascination, captivated by the way the light plays on her diaphanous skin, by the mystery dancing in her dark eyes, by the delicate curve of her neck when she leans over the board.

At one point, overwhelmed by the intensity of his emotions, Jun dares to raise his eyes to her, seeking in her gaze a confirmation of the bond that seems to be forming between them. She answers his look, her deep eyes capturing his with a disturbing intensity. You play remarkably well, she says in a melodious voice that seems to carry within it echoes of another time.

It is you who are guiding me, Jun replies sincerely, aware that his words carry a double meaning he makes no attempt to hide.

She leans slightly towards him, an enigmatic gleam in her eyes. Are you certain that it is really ōgi we are playing, Jun?

His heart pounding, he murmurs: I am playing for the privilege of sharing this moment with you.

The air around them seems to vibrate with an electric tension, the park itself holding its breath as it waits for her answer. Her smile softens, revealing an unexpected vulnerability that only heightens her bewitching charm. In her eyes is reflected a deep understanding, as if she were reading Jun like an open book, discovering there truths of which he himself is unaware.
