Chapter 15
The Murmur of Doubts

The lobby of the complex hummed with a peculiar energy that morning. Takeshi's appearance sent a ripple through the crowd of journalists, their bodies pressing forward like an inexorable wave. The air filled with the clicking of cameras and the rustle of notebooks.

A young woman with delicate features broke away from the group, her press badge swinging to the rhythm of her steps. Her voice, clear and assured, cut through the surrounding buzz: Champion Takeshi... The words hung in the air, awaiting an answer that did not come.

Other questions followed, striking the champion's silence like raindrops on a windowpane. A veteran of the sports press, his greying temples betraying years spent hunting for the flaws of champions, scrutinised Takeshi's face with an almost palpable intensity. His expression betrayed the instinct of a predator before wounded prey.

Takeshi walked on, his body tracing a straight line through the mass of journalists, who parted in spite of themselves. His silence was not the usual one of a champion focused on his next challenge. There was something deeper in his fixed gaze, as if his thoughts were wandering in spaces the journalists' questions could not reach.

Flashes crackled, capturing his unreadable expression, his measured step, the subtle tension in his shoulders. Every image would feed the speculation; every silence would be dissected in the columns of the evening papers. But no lens could capture what was really at stake behind that impassive mask.

The hubbub gradually faded as he neared the doors leading to the competition halls. The last questions died in the air, giving way to a silence more eloquent than any answer he could have given. In that silence, Takeshi's footsteps rang out like a metronome, marking the tempo of a melody only he could hear.
