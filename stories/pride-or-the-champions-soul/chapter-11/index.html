Chapter 11
The Opponent's Mask

Reality seemed to warp at the edges of his vision. The ōgi board, until then the anchor of his consciousness, wavered between crystalline clarity and an unsettling blur. The pieces, arranged in a configuration that promised him victory, now seemed as distant as a mirage in the desert. The mathematical certainty of his triumph was crumbling before the physical impossibility of sustaining his concentration.

Raising his eyes from the board for the first time in what felt like an eternity, Takeshi searched his opponent's face for a sign of fatigue akin to his own. What he found froze him where he sat. The woman in the blue kimono was contemplating him with a serenity that defied all logic, as if time and effort had no hold on her.

Their eyes met, and an involuntary shiver ran down the champion's spine. In the half-light of the carriage, lit only by the wan glow of the reading lamps and the silver glints of the moon, her smile changed. It was no longer the polite expression of a respectful opponent, but something older, more primal. A smile that seemed to feed on his growing distress.

The light played strange tricks on her face, hollowing shadows where there should have been none, creating impossible angles in the geometry of her features. Her pupils held unfathomable depths, as if behind her eyes there stretched not the interior of the carriage but an endless abyss.
