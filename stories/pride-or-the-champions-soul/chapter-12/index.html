Chapter 12
The King's Abdication

An almost tangible silence pervaded the carriage, punctuated only by the rhythmic glide of the train on the rails. The spectators, frozen in collective stillness, seemed to be holding their breath before the spectacle playing out in front of them. In that suspended instant, a sudden clarity cut through Takeshi's clouded mind.

His hand found one of his captured pawns, that humble piece which, between his trembling fingers, suddenly seemed to bear the weight of all his pent-up rage. He lifted it slowly, deliberately, like a priest preparing to perform a profane sacrifice.

I resign.

The words cracked in the silence, sharp as a blade. With a brutal, almost savage gesture, he brought the pawn down on the opposing King, crushing it against the board as one exterminates a noxious insect. The blow rang through the whole carriage, a sharp, violent sound that made the spectators start. The board shook under the impact, sending out a shock wave that set the pieces dancing before they collapsed in a cascade.

The pieces fell one by one, some rolling to the floor in a chorus of dull thuds, others sliding under the neighbouring seats like so many fallen soldiers fleeing a battlefield. The King, target of this cathartic violence, lay overturned on the board, a silent witness to this sacrilege against the sacred rules of ōgi.

In the violence of that gesture lay all the frustration of a cornered champion, who would rather destroy the game than submit to its implacable rules.

The horrified murmurs of the spectators rose like a swarm, but Takeshi no longer heard them. Rising with deliberate slowness, he walked away from the field of ruins, leaving behind him not only a butchered game but also the scattered fragments of his dignity as a champion.
