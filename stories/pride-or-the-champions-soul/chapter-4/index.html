Chapter 4
The Dance of Pieces

The first minutes of play passed in a silence broken only by the muffled click of the pieces on the board. Despite his tiredness, Takeshi deployed his usual strategies, his fingers tracing on the board the patterns that had made his name. The game promised to be like so many others—or so he believed.

His opponent's opening defied his expectations. Unconventional moves, yet strangely effective, like a dissonant melody suddenly finding its coherence. Her moves seemed to anticipate his own, turning each exchange into an intricate dance in which the initiative changed hands with disconcerting fluidity.

As the game progressed, a troubling realisation forced itself upon Takeshi. This woman did not play like an amateur—nor even like a professional. Her style transcended the usual categories, as if she had access to an understanding of the game that still eluded him. Her fingers seemed barely to brush the pieces, and yet each move shook the foundations of his best-established strategies.

A new tension settled in the back of his neck, a physical reminder of the unusual mental effort this game demanded. His initial irritation at this unforeseen distraction was gradually turning into something deeper—a mixture of reluctant admiration and growing unease.
