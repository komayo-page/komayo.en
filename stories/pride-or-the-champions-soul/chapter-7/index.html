Chapter 7
The Shadows of Doubt

The carriage, until then sunk in nocturnal drowsiness, was stirring with new life. Figures took shape in the half-light, drawn by the silent ballet being played out on the ōgi board. Phone screens glowed like fireflies in the dark, their lenses furtively capturing the scene.

This growing attention created an invisible bubble around the duel, an intangible border between the ordinary world of the train and the universe unfolding on the ōgiban. The impromptu spectators held their breath, as if the slightest murmur might shatter the precarious balance of this suspended moment.

Takeshi, though accustomed to the spotlights of great competitions, felt this proximity differently. The forced intimacy of the carriage turned every gaze into a physical presence, every movement of the crowd into a groundswell eroding his concentration. The space seemed to contract around the board, creating an almost palpable density.

But it was the mystery emanating from his opponent that truly occupied his thoughts. Her play, defying all conventional classification, raised questions that piled up like the pieces of an impossible puzzle. The mastery she displayed matched no known style, no recorded school. Her moves seemed to draw their inspiration from a source older and deeper than the mere tradition of ōgi.
